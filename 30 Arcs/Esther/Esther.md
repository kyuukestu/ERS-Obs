## Stage 1: Dreamer

Current.

- Optimistic.
- Dramatic.
- Ambitious.
- Loves attention.
- Wants to help everyone.

---

## Stage 2: Rising Star

Future.

- Success arrives.
- Crowds adore her.
- Fame grows.

Empathy begins creating a feedback loop.

She literally feels admiration.

---

## Stage 3: Dependency

Crowd approval becomes emotional fuel.

The danger isn't fame.

It's chemically feeling thousands of people cheering.

---

## Stage 4: Collapse

One disastrous battle.

Crowd turns.

Admiration becomes condemnation.

She experiences emotional whiplash.

---

## Stage 5: Obsession

After Oblivia suffers.

Validation no longer enough.

Protection becomes everything.

---

## Stage 6: Vigilante

Extra-judicial actions.

Punishment.

Retribution.

Isolation.

---

## Stage 7: Reconstruction

Learns:

- Boundaries.
- Delegation.
- Trust.
- Partnership.

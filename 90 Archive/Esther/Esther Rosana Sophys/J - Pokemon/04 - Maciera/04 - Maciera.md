#### Name Origin

_**Capoeira**_

Afro-Brazilian martial art that draws from dance, acrobatics, music, and spirituality. It is known for its acrobatic and complex maneuvers, often involving hands on the ground and inverted kicks. It emphasizes flowing movements rather than fixed stances; the ginga, a rocking step, is usually the focal point of the technique.

_**Engola**_

Meaning ‘strength/power’, a performance of Angolan ritual combat, thought to be one of the traditions Capoeira draws from.

_**Maculelê**_

Afro-Brazilian mock combat dance that utilizes stick as weapons. Traditionally performed by two people in dried grass skirts.

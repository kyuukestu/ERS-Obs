
## Maçeira En’Çapolalê / “Ciera” (Bounsweet → Tsareena)

### Core Identity

- Defiant underdog
- Learned to fight because weakness invited cruelty

### Personality

- Proud, scrappy, confrontational
- Takes insults personally
- Fiercely independent
- Develops a cruel streak as she gains power

### Existing Arc Beats

- Found fighting Tyrogue
- Resents being preyed upon
- Relies on recovery and endurance
- Battles brutally for a Bounsweet

### Future Arc

- Evolves into increasingly dominant Tsareena
- Begins enjoying punishment/control
- Esther gradually realizes confidence can curdle into sadism
- Parallel to Esther’s own moral drift

### Symbolism

- Victims becoming bullies
- Power as emotional compensation
- The intoxicating nature of superiority

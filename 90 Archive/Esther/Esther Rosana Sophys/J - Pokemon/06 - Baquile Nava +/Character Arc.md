## Nava (Goomy → Goodra)

### Core Identity

- Esther’s “baby”
- Vulnerable dragon under Esther’s protection

### Personality

- Affectionate, trusting, soft
- Dependent on care
- Quietly brave

### Existing Arc Beats

- Coddled heavily
- Esther learns hard lessons caring for him
- Represents Esther’s nurturing side

### Future Arc

- Evolves into Goodra
- Has triumphant moment protecting Esther
- Potential death

### Symbolism

- Innocence
- Esther’s capacity for tenderness
- Protection becoming sacrifice

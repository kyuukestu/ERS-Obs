
## Pyrrha Achilleia Astierra (Jangmo-o → Hakamo-o → Kommo-o)

### Core Identity

- Esther’s partner Pokémon
- Mother-child coded relationship
- Aggressive, proud, emotionally intense
- Defines herself through strength and battle

### Personality

- Courageous and combative
- Deep fear of abandonment
- Emotionally dependent on Esther’s approval
- Prone to rage and over-escalation
- Wants to make Esther proud

### Existing Arc Beats

- Earned her name through fearless battling
- Injured Esther during a rampage
- Withdrew emotionally afterward
- Empathic bond & Telepathy via Munna repaired their relationship
- Learned Scale Shot alongside Esther
- Esther began over-relying on her
- Esther & Pyrrha intentionally delay evolution over fears of what Hakamo-o could become. Pyrrha needs more time to mature.

### Future Arc

- Reassert herself independently
- Secretly train Dual Chop under a mentor
- Hakamo-o phase mirrors Esther’s growing aggression
- Kommo-o evolution tied to restraint and maturity
- Shared lesson:
    
    > strength is knowing _when_ to fight
    

### Symbolism

- Esther’s emotional volatility
- Validation through strength
- The danger of crowd-fueled aggression
- Loyalty becoming self-destructive devotion

### Custom Ball

- Legionnaire Ball
- Roman military motifs
- Discipline over fury

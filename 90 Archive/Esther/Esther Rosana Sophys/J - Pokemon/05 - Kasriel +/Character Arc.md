## Kasriel (Galarian Zigzagoon → Obstagoon)

### Core Identity

- Rakish gremlin with a good heart

### Personality

- Relaxed, flirty, troublesome
- Loves fighting as “play”
- Mischievous but loyal

### Existing Arc Beats

- Repeatedly challenged Pyrrha
- Became recurring companion
- Eventually joined Esther willingly

### Future Arc

- Evolves into protective Obstagoon
- Likely dies defending Esther or the team
- Death marks shift from adventure to tragedy

### Symbolism

- Joyful rebellion
- The cost of loyalty
- Levity disappearing from Esther’s life

# Protect

I have observed that different Pokemon demonstrate different forms of the move Protect with significant deviation in the appearance of each form. Nothing I have observed leads me to believe these variations are unique to species or individual. Thus far, at least three variants of Protect have been identified:
## Variants
### Shield Var
A limited single-directional Protect. I'm not sure what the benefit of this one is. Protect is fast but... doesn't this leave the Pokemon open to flanking attacks?  My best guess is that the defensive power gets more... concentrated(?). Shayan said a wide enough gulf between foes and a strong enough attack could *shatter* Protect; maybe this is a some kind of counter-measure to that.

### Dome Var
This is Pyrrha’s natural style of Protect—projecting a dome of energy that guards against attacks. It’s the largest of the forms—(maybe it consumes more energy?)—and is able to protect both the user and anything within their immediate vicinity.

Coordinator's Armaldo Protect - Nando
![Armaldo Protect](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/3/3b/Nando_Armaldo_Protect.png)

### Aura Var
Aura's my guess—I don't really know what this is. It doesn't look as *solid* as the other two variants but that's no indication of its strength. It seems plenty potent. Maybe this is the ultimate form of protect—it provides full-body coverage and is concentrated into a layer just above the skin... to preserve energy?

# Esther's Mentality Pre-Vigilante
Esther's already a bit cocksure and her rising fame and fervent fan-base largely causes double-down as her ego inflates. Play up the hero complex. She feels like she's the best for the job and assumes complete responsibility (especially with her brother out of the picture). She's tunnel-visions on doing it all herself and is basically being crushed under the weight of an impossibly large task.

I think this ties in strongly to Pyrrha's arc of dealing with her more aggressive instincts because when she (as a Hakamo-o) lashes out and goes too far in battles it puts a lot more scrutiny and criticism on Esther who generally goes out of her way to present/perform for the crowd.

There's a specific fan-art I wanted to find from the POV of a Trainer who got hit by a Pokemon move in battle and I think that's generally the vibe I'm going for? A situation where the battle goes too far and the crowd turns against her so-to-speak.

The Vigilante Arc comes out specifically because she's under immense stress/pressure and at the same time feels isolated. She has to do it all by herself and her only option is to go to 'extremes'.
